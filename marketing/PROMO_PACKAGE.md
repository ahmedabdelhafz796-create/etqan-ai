# E-tqan — Promotional Package

A complete, production-ready brief for a **2–3 minute cinematic promo video**
plus social cut-downs. Everything an editor or an AI video pipeline needs:
storyboard, Arabic voice-over script, burned-in subtitles, editing
instructions, animation/generation prompts, and platform versions.

Brand: dark luxury, ivory + gold, emerald/royal/violet/cyan accents.
Fonts: Sora (display), Source Serif 4 (serif), Inter (UI), Cairo (Arabic).
Tone: aspirational, confident, calm — "the skills that actually pay."

## 4. Editing Instructions

- **Aspect masters**: 16:9 (YouTube/site), 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), 1:1 (feed).
- **Grade**: deep near-black blacks, warm gold highlights, slight film grain,
  subtle vignette. Avoid pure white — use ivory (#FCFBF7).
- **Motion**: ease-in-out only (cubic 0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). No linear moves.
- **Cut rhythm**: sync hard cuts to the music's downbeats in section 3.
- **Type animation**: text blurs in from y+20 over 0.5s; gold words use a
  left-to-right shimmer sweep once.
- **Audio**: -14 LUFS master; VO ducks music by 6 dB; add UI foley (click,
  chime) on the checkout beat.
- **Logo sting**: monogram draws on via stroke reveal, then fills gold gradient.

  at 2:22. (Suno/Udio prompt: "cinematic inspirational, warm, minimal piano and
  pads, building, hopeful, premium tech brand, 150s".)
