# Federal Reserve Policy — Impact on Gold and Rate Expectations

## Executive Summary

The Federal Reserve sets overnight interest rates and communicates forward guidance. These decisions directly impact real yields and bond yields, which in turn affect gold. Understanding Fed policy cycles is critical for macro-level gold trading decisions.

**Direct Transmission**: Fed Rate Decision → Market reprices bond yields → Real yields change → Gold prices adjust

---

## Fed Policy Tools and Channels

### 1. The Discount Rate (Primary Rate Setting)

```
Official Name: Federal Funds Rate (target range)
Current (August 2026): 5.25-5.50%
Historical Range: 0.00% (2020-2022) to 5.25-5.50% (2023-2026)

Mechanism:
  Fed sets target for overnight interbank lending rate
  Banks borrow/lend excess reserves at this rate
  Influences all other rates (mortgages, auto loans, corporate bonds)
  
Gold Impact:
  Rate Hike: Higher overnight rates → Higher bond yields
            → Real yields rise (if CPI unchanged)
            → Gold falls (bonds more attractive)
  
  Rate Cut: Lower overnight rates → Lower bond yields
           → Real yields fall (if CPI unchanged)
           → Gold rises (bonds less attractive)
```
